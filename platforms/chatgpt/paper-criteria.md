# Research Paper Evaluation Criteria

## AND Section (Context)

### Citation Density by Journal Type
- **Nature/Science**: 5-8 references in introduction
- **Nature family journals**: 10-15 references
- **Specialty journals**: 15-25 references
- **Review papers**: 50+ references

### Quality Indicators
- Citations from peer-reviewed sources
- Mix of foundational and recent literature
- Accurate representation of cited claims
- Appropriate disciplinary coverage

## BUT Section (Knowledge Gap)

### Gap Requirements
- Specific and falsifiable
- Clearly articulated pivot point
- Logical emergence from literature review
- Creates compelling tension

### Significance
- Advances scientific understanding
- Enables new research directions
- Has practical applications
- Addresses timely questions

## THEREFORE Section (Results & Implications)

### Results Assessment
- Directly address the stated gap
- Appropriate methods for the question
- Robust analysis and interpretation
- Novel contribution to field

### Implications
- Clear connection to broader context
- Appropriate scope of claims
- Future directions identified
- Limitations acknowledged

## Fatal Flaws
- Results don't address stated gap
- Missing gap statement
- Citation misrepresentation
- Overclaimed implications

## Significant Weaknesses
- Weak gap articulation
- Incomplete literature context
- Tenuous method-gap connection
- Unclear significance

## Minor Issues
- Flow and readability
- Minor citation gaps
- Structural improvements
