# Research Proposal Evaluation Criteria

## AND Section (Context)

### Citation Density Guidelines
- **NSFC proposals**: 15-25 references typical
- **NSF proposals**: 20-40 references typical
- **Introduction section**: 8-15 key works

### Quality Indicators
- Citations are peer-reviewed and from reputable journals
- Recent literature included (within 5 years)
- Foundational works cited alongside recent advances
- Citations accurately represent source claims

## BUT Section (Knowledge Gap)

### Gap Requirements
- Must be specific and clearly articulated
- Must be fundable—addressable within project timeline
- Must emerge logically from the AND section
- Should create genuine tension demanding resolution

### Significance Assessment
- Scientific advancement potential
- Technology development implications
- Policy relevance
- Societal impact

## THEREFORE Section (Proposed Methods)

### Method-Gap Alignment
- "Glove-to-hand" fit between problem and approach
- Clear rationale for methodological choices
- Feasibility evidence (preliminary data, pilot studies)
- Innovation beyond existing approaches

### Expected Outcomes
- Specific, measurable deliverables
- Timeline-appropriate scope
- Clear contribution to filling the stated gap

## Fatal Flaws (Likely Rejection)
- Missing or vague gap statement
- Methods don't address the stated problem
- Citation misrepresents source material
- Unfundable scope (too broad/narrow)

## Significant Weaknesses
- Gap significance unclear
- Incomplete literature coverage
- Weak method justification
- Missing feasibility evidence

## Minor Issues
- Phrasing and flow improvements
- Minor citation additions
- Structural reorganization suggestions
