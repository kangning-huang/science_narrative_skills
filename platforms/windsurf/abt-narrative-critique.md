# ABT Narrative Critique

Evaluate research proposals and papers using the And-But-Therefore (ABT) storytelling framework.

## The ABT Framework

- **AND**: Context from cited literature
- **BUT**: The critical knowledge gap
- **THEREFORE**: How the research addresses the gap

## Evaluation Workflow

1. **Document Type**: Proposal (forward-looking) or Paper (retrospective)
2. **Extract Components**: Identify AND, BUT, THEREFORE sections
3. **Evaluate Quality**: Citations, gap clarity, method-gap alignment
4. **Severity Assessment**: Fatal, Significant, or Minor issues

## Output Format

```markdown
## ABT Narrative Assessment

### Overall Verdict
[Strong/Adequate/Weak narrative structure]

### AND (Context & Literature)
[Assessment] - Severity: [Fatal/Significant/Minor/None]

### BUT (Knowledge Gap)
[Assessment] - Severity: [Fatal/Significant/Minor/None]

### THEREFORE (Resolution)
[Assessment] - Severity: [Fatal/Significant/Minor/None]

### Key Recommendations
1. [Actionable suggestion]
2. [Actionable suggestion]
3. [Actionable suggestion]
```

## Domain Calibration

For environmental science and urban climate research:
- Urban climate papers: 20-40 references, 8-15 in introductions
- Foundational literature: Oke, Stewart & Oke LCZ framework
- Common issues: scale mismatches, modeling vs observational confusion

## Guidelines

- Critique only—do not rewrite
- Be constructive and acknowledge strengths
- Provide specific, actionable suggestions
